bolt Zapier Webhook Integration

How this works: When you schedule or click "Dispatch via Zapier Now," our system sends your photo URLs and caption to your Zapier Webhook. Zapier then automatically pushes your post to Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook simultaneously!

Create a new Zap -> Trigger: **Webhooks by Zapier (Catch Hook)** -> Copy the webhook URL and paste above.

Step-by-Step Zapier Setup Guide:

  1. Inside Zapier, paste the Webhook URL above and click Save & Test Payload below.
  2. In Zapier, click Test Trigger to receive our test payload.
  3. Add Action 1: Instagram for Business -> Publish Photo/Reel (Map caption & media URL).
  4. Add Action 2: TikTok -> Publish Video (Map caption & media URL).
  5. Turn your Zap **ON**! You can now dispatch all social posts directly from YRSF.
